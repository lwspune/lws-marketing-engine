# nda-life-001-campus storyboard

@lws.pune's first **NDA life** reel (framework F41, idea N1 in `lwspune-ideas.md`; lesson L79). NDA as a toy-like 3D
miniature, flown over like a drone; a question with a real answer turns the tour into a guess. Not a maths concept, so
the maths-only points are adapted or set aside in § 0 (as `nda-defence-001-army-ranks`). **Revised after the review
(§ 10).**

## 1. The idea
- One sentence: the viewer sees NDA from above, its three famous places, and learns that the passing-out parade is on
  **Khetarpal Ground**, where the passing-out course crosses the **Antim Pag** (the last step).
- End card: **It starts with the written exam. / Maths: 300 of 900 marks.** (F43). Chapter: NDA life, the campus.
- Source idea: N1 (founder's pick, 2026-11-02). No PYQ.

## 2. The viewer
- An NDA aspirant, 16–19, scrolling, often muted. Knows Sudan Block on sight (on every NDA poster); has seen parade
  photos with the dome in the background.
- Most will answer **A Sudan Block**: the building they know, and it's in the parade photos. **C Peacock Bay** pulls
  anyone who half-remembers it from NDA videos. **B Khetarpal** is the name fewer know: the reel teaches it. The chip
  says only "Khetarpal" so the word "Ground" doesn't give it away (review B1).

## 3. The question (frame 0)
- `NDA LIFE · THE CAMPUS`, then 2 lines: "Where is the passing-out" / "parade held?" (≤ 34 characters each)
- Chips in one row: **A Sudan Block · B Khetarpal · C Peacock Bay**, then "Comment your guess 👇".
- Everything needed is on frame 0: the three options, and on the model a pin over each place in its chip's colour.
  A recall question (you know it or you guess), answerable in 2 s.

## 4. Frame 0 and the cover
- Frame 0: the whole miniature from ~35° above, in late-afternoon gold light, with soft tilt-shift blur at the top and
  bottom of the picture band (the "toy model" look): hills around, the lake on one side with sailing boats already
  gliding, Sudan Block's red dome at the centre, the open green ground in front of it, birds crossing. Three pins (A, B,
  C) bob above their places. The orbit is **at speed on frame 0** (eases out only).
- The ground is **empty** until 9.5 s: no marching blocks before the question has had its time.
- Cover at **t = 2.6 s**: all three pins in view, no ✗ or ✓, the ground empty. The question repeated on the cover
  mid-page (y 600–900) so the 3:4 grid crop (y 240–1680) keeps it whole (review I5).

## 7. The story without words
A tiny, perfect model of a hilly campus by a lake, turning. Three markers on three places. The camera visits a grand
domed building: its marker is struck out. It visits the lake with sailing boats: struck out. It visits a huge green
ground in front of the domed building, where little uniformed figures march in, in blocks: the marker lights up. The
last blocks march out and step, rank by rank, over a glowing line while the rest stand still. Jets roar over. The camera
rises to the whole campus at sunset. Each beat reads with the words hidden; the line's name (Antim Pag) needs its word,
but the picture still shows "the leavers cross a last line".

## 9. Risks
- **The model reads as a game map, not NDA.** Sudan Block's red dome (One Day's model, already recognised) is the
  first close-up; the pill names it.
- **Someone who knows NDA says "the lake isn't there".** The caption's "our own model, not to scale"; the tilt-shift and
  the moving camera keep it from reading as a map.
- **The answer shows early.** The ground empty until 9.5 s; the chip says "Khetarpal", not "Khetarpal Ground", until
  the ✓; the cover at 2.6 s. Test: no marching figure, no ✓ and no "Ground" before 9.5 s.
- **A caption read against the wrong place.** Each ✗ caption and its pill hold 0.7–0.8 s before the next pill (test:
  the pill never changes while a ✗ caption is less than 0.6 s old).
- **Tiny figures flicker or alias.** At least ~12 px tall in the parade shots; instanced meshes; checked on the stills.
- **Jets read as birds / too small, or as Surya Kiran.** A low pass with the wings visible, ≥ 1/4 of the frame wide at
  the closest point, no smoke.
- **Header too tall or text in the button zones.** Measured on the stills; caption and end-card lines ≤ 840 px, tested.
- **The model clips at the orbit's corners.** ≤ 960 px wide at every angle, tested.

## 11. Build notes (after the review panel's pass 1, 2026-11-03)

What changed from the plan above, and why. The times in `remotion/src/lib/life001.ts`'s beat table are the current ones.
- **Picture band 470–1700** (was 470–1420): the picture runs under the captions and the pill, which sit on white cards
  (Drama and Colour: "a third of the frame is empty cream"). Text still stays above y 1600 (tested).
- **Pins one dark colour** with a white letter (Colour: green on the grass and blue on the water vanished; A's orange beside
  B's green was a colour-blind pair). Each pops in turn in the opening (0.3, 0.9, 1.5 s: a new event every 0.6 s, Motion),
  and with its mark: a big red ✗ or green ✓ disc (all three: the marks were ~20 px). A ring flashes out from B on the answer.
  The answer chip lights in the ✓'s green, not the board's amber (Colour, Drama).
- **Antim Pag:** a 2.4 m saturated gold band on a dark edge that flashes as each rank crosses (Drama, Colour: the hairline vanished).
- **Cadets** a third bigger, with swinging legs and thick white belts (Motion: "barcodes"). Still plain navy, no uniform claimed.
- **Boats:** all sails white and the camera never within 70 m of a boat (all three: a near orange sail read as a red shard).
- **Jets:** a camera set off the jets' path on the side away from the lake; they fly towards it in a 30 m vee and pass close
  (Drama, Motion: they overlapped and left the frame). Pale wingtip streaks, no smoke.
- **The sunset and the "dome lights" beats are cut** (all three: neither showed at that size). The light warms and the
  shadows lengthen in the pull-back; the end card's three lines arrive one by one.
- **Wide view from 64°** so the slab fills the band (≤ 960 px wide at every angle, tested); the closing view sits above the
  end card (tested).
- Declined: "open in the middle of the action" (Drama): the options are places on the model, so frame 0 shows the model
  (F4), and every 3D reel opens on the orbit (F22).
