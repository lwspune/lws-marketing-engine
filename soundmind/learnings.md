# @soundmindwithshivani learnings log

Kept apart from the LWS logs (`reels/learnings.md`, `reels/pyqvault-learnings.md`): an outside brand, a different
audience and different formats (carousels and "ME" story reels). Same method: every lesson from real numbers becomes a
**testable idea**: what we saw, what we think it means, which posts test it, and the result. Update **Result** and
**Status** after each post's 7-day numbers (GoPost: the post's Done card, or Insights). A point in `framework.md` (the
S-numbers) changes only through its change log.

All three logs share **one numbering** (L1, L2, …), so a number always means one lesson. The next new lesson is **L57**,
in whichever log it belongs to.

**Status:** 🧪 testing · ✅ confirmed (do it from now on) · ❌ rejected (don't) · 📌 fact (no test needed)

Baseline (all 81 Sound Mind reels to 2026-10-21, `analytics/soundmindwithshivani-reels-2026-10-21.csv`): **2.7 shares and
1.8 saves per 1,000 people reached**; the median reel gets **390 views · 196 reach · 0 shares · 0 saves · 6.1 s watched**.
26 of 81 reels got at least one share, 31 at least one save; the top 10 % got 3 shares or more. A post wins when it
beats 2.7 shares per 1,000 reached with at least 2 shares, at 7 days (`framework.md` → Winning means).
