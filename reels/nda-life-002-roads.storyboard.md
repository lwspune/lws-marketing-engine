# nda-life-002-roads storyboard: 3 roads after NDA

@lws.pune's second **NDA life** reel (framework F41, idea N5 in `lwspune-ideas.md`). A **showcase** (F4 v1.17): a small 3D
diorama with NDA at the front; three roads run out of its gate: one north into the mountains (the Indian Military Academy,
Dehradun), one north to the sea (the Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala), one east to an airfield (the Air Force Academy,
Hyderabad). A column of cadets marches down each road from frame 0; the camera picks up each column as it arrives, the
academy is named and the rank a year later is said, and each arrival has its own moment (the academy's flag runs up its mast; the ship
sounds its horn and turns; a trainer plane takes off). The camera pulls up over all three glowing roads.

## 1. The idea
- One sentence: **after 3 years at NDA, cadets go on to their own service's academy**: Army cadets to the Indian Military
  Academy, Dehradun; Naval cadets to the Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala; Air Force cadets to the Air Force Academy,
  Hyderabad; about a year later they are Lieutenants, Sub-Lieutenants and Flying Officers.
- Why: most aspirants know "NDA" but not what comes after it; the answer is a picture (three roads, three landscapes) and
  makes NDA the start of a career, not the end of an exam. Saved and shared ("which road is yours?" in the post's caption).
- Not a choice made at NDA: the service is put in order of preference in the UPSC application (the post's caption says
  so, review I6).

## 2. The viewer
- NDA aspirant 16–19 and their parents, often muted. Knows the exam, not the path after it.

## 3. Frame 0
- Header: `NDA LIFE · THE ROAD AHEAD`, then "After 3 years at NDA," / "3 roads to 3 academies", then three numbered dots
  (1–3) in their road's colour: the road being shown fills, shown ones go dark.
- Picture: a square diorama seen ¾ from above, the camera already turning: NDA at the front (the domed Sudan Block model, a
  gate); three roads in three colours (Army gold on the green hills, Navy deep blue on the sand, Air Force sky blue on the
  grey-tan plain, apart in lightness as well as hue; each tested against its own ground and for colour-blind viewers, review I3, panel pass 1) drawing out of the gate ahead of
  three columns of 9 cadets already marching: left, up into green hills that rise to snowy peaks with pines; straight back,
  down to a beach and a blue sea with a grey ship sailing; right, to a flat plain with a runway, two hangars and a small
  trainer plane. A small academy building at each road's end; a number tag (1–3) pops over each.

## 7. The story without words
A small school sits at the front of a model landscape. Three groups of cadets march out of its gate on three roads: to the
mountains, to the sea, to an airfield. Each reaches a new academy: its flag runs up, a ship sounds its horn, a plane
takes off. From high above, all three roads glow.

## 9. Risks
- **Busy, toy-like.** One diorama, three clearly different lands (green-and-snow, blue-and-sand, grey runway), soft light,
  tilt-shift blur on the edges (as the campus tour).
- **Small cadets.** Big toy figures (about 5 m on the model) so the camera can follow close.
- **Three similar visits.** Each ends on its own event: the flag running up, the horn and the ship's turn, the take-off.

## 11. Review panel, pass 1 (F38, 2026-11-03): what changed
- **The mist is gone:** it lifted off screen. Instead the IMA's flag runs up its mast as column 1 arrives (tested: it rises in
  the picture).
- **The ship** sails in beside the INA, not behind it (tested: ≥ 260 px, ≤ 15 % hidden by the building, from the horn on). Its
  horn moved to 13.3 s, with steam puffs; it is quieter (it had been 9 dB over the music).
- **The trainer** is bigger, noses up at wheels-up (17.4 s) and crosses ≥ 500 px; the engine builds without a gap.
- **Colours:** gold, deep blue and sky blue, apart in lightness, so colour-blind viewers can tell them apart. The header dots
  and pills match their roads.
- **Peaks** stand on the board, so they read as mountains, not smoke. Roads start part-drawn. Markers under each column in
  the wide opening. The camera arcs while it follows.
- **Sound:** one whoosh at the pass, not three; marching on the beat; waves only at the coast; a soft sound on the end card.
- **Declined:** a closer opening (the whole diorama is the promise, F22), a shorter end card (3 s is the house length), a
  darker stage (the white board, F12) and bigger header dots (a shared component). Logged in `review-panel.md`.

## 12. Review panel, pass 2 (F38, 2026-11-03, the last pass): what changed

  off mid-frame at 17.9 s, and is still climbing as the camera pulls up (tested: whole in frame through the roll and wheels-up,
  clear of the column). It had rolled and lifted off past the right edge.
- **The IMA's flag reads:** a bigger flag (6.4 × 4 m on the model), turned across the camera's view, run up once the camera

- **A pale parade square** where each column halts, so the navy uniforms stand out (the Navy column had vanished into its
  deep-blue road; tested for contrast).
- **No tree** between the camera and the ship at its horn, or the trainer's roll and lift-off.
- **Sound:** a shallow music dip that starts once the engine has built; five engine layers that build fast and fade over
  2.4 s into the pull-up; the horn and shimmers under the music; a click for "4 years: cadet to officer."; surf only on the
  coast; wind and birds quieter (tested).
