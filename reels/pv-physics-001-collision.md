## The physics (checked)

- Equal 1 kg balls, A at 4 m/s into B at rest, elastic. Contact modelled as a soft spring: the relative
  velocity turns as u·cos φ (φ: 0 → π), so momentum is exactly 4 kg·m/s throughout and kinetic energy dips
  into the squash (max 4 J at mid-contact) and returns: total 8 J always.
- Why A stops: after the hit (vA, vB) must satisfy vA + vB = 4 (momentum, a line) and vA² + vB² = 16
  (energy, a circle). A line meets a circle in at most two points: (4, 0) = before, (0, 4) = after.
- JEE Main 2022 Q8: share of KE passed to a ball M = 5m at rest = 4mM/(m + M)² = 20/36 = 55.6% → **(c)**.
  Live: ball 1 rebounds at −2.67 m/s (44.4%), ball 5 leaves at 1.33 m/s (55.6%). Trap: (a) 50% assumes an
  even split; only equal masses pass on 100%.
