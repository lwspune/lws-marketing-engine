# LWS Marketing Engine — Master Plan

> Your single source of truth. Read section 0, ignore the rest until you need it.
> The job: **bring buyers to every LWS product** (PYQ Vault first, Crack SSB second, more later)
> with a routine that costs you about two hours a week.

---

## 0. The whole thing in 6 lines

1. Every product gets a **profile**: who buys it, the real problems they say out loud, what you
   already have that answers those problems, your price, your voice.
2. Each week the app drafts that product's content **from its profile**: silent pen-and-paper Reel
   scripts, captions, Telegram and WhatsApp posts, YouTube titles.
3. You **approve, tweak, or kill** each piece in seconds. Nothing goes out without you.
4. Approved pieces export as one batch you paste into the free scheduling tools.
5. Later: a free gift per product captures leads into Telegram/WhatsApp, and a written-once
   follow-up sequence sells.
6. One number tells you if it works: **leads captured per week, per product.**

That loop, run weekly per product, is the whole system.

---

## 1. Why this shape

- **Product profiles are the foundation.** Everything the app writes is grounded in a profile.
  That is what stops the output being generic study-tip slop. A piece that cannot name a real
  student problem and a real thing you have is a failure, not a feature.
- **You are one person.** The bottleneck was never ideas. It was producing and shipping content
  consistently for several products. A weekly batch plus a fast approval gate fixes that.
- **Faceless, silent-first.** Core format is pen-and-paper solving filmed top-down, steps as
  on-screen text, music, no face, no voice. Voice is an optional later upgrade.
- **You approve everything.** AI drafts; you decide. Never fully automatic publishing.

## 2. What it is not

- Not an SEO tool. PYQ Vault's indexing problem is a separate job inside that app's own codebase.
- Not a social media scheduler. Free tools already do that.
- Not a product to sell to other coaches. Prove it brings you buyers first.
