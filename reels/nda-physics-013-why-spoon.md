## The idea (checked)

- The inside of a spoon is a concave mirror; here f = 20 cm (F), C = 40 cm. Mirror formula with distances in
  front positive: 1/v = 1/f − 1/u, magnification m = −v/u.
- u = 100 → v = 25, m = −0.25 (small, upside down, near F) · u = 40 → v = 40, m = −1 (at C, same size) ·
  u = 25 → v = 100, m = −4 · u = 20 → no image (rays parallel) · u = 10 → v = −20 (behind), m = +2 (upright).
- On screen the two rays are drawn from the geometry (one parallel → through F, one at the pole → equal
  angle) and the image is where they meet, so the picture and the live numbers agree.
- NDA 2026 (I) GAT Q64: object from far beyond C towards the mirror → image moves from F to C. Image speed
  = (v/u)² × object speed; (v/u)² rises from 0 to 1 and the object is accelerating → the image accelerates
  towards C → **(a)**. Traps: (b) F is where the image *starts*; (c) the pole; (d) the speed isn't constant.
