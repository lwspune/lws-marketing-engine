## The idea (checked)

- An n×n square becomes (n+1)×(n+1) by adding an L of **2n + 1** tiles: 1, then +3, +5, +7, …
- So 1 + 3 + 5 + … + (2n − 1) = **n²** (1 = 1², 1 + 3 = 4 = 2², …, up to 10 × 10 = 100 on screen).
- NDA 2026 (I) Q20: Mₖ = [[k, k−1], [k−1, k]] → |Mₖ| = k² − (k−1)² = **2k − 1**, so |M₁| + … + |M₅₀| =
  1 + 3 + … + 99 = **50² = 2500** → **(c)**. Traps: (a) 50 = every determinant taken as 1; (b) 1250 and
  (d) 5000 = half and double of 50².
