- After 3 years at NDA, Army cadets go to the IMA, Dehradun; Naval cadets to the INA, Ezhimala; Air Force cadets to the
  AFA, Hyderabad (Dundigal). A year later: Lieutenant; Sub-Lieutenant; Flying Officer (pilots after one year of basic
  flying training, Ground Duties at the end of AFA training; NDA's first women cadets were commissioned at AFA a year after
  passing out).

- Our own generic buildings and faceless figures: no crests, no insignia, no uniforms; a diorama, not a map.
