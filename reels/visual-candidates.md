# Visual reel candidates

NDA Maths PYQs whose answer **is a picture**: the figure makes the answer obvious, not just
decorative. Pick visual reels from here (`visual-reel-workflow.md`). Re-derive each answer before
making the reel; the descriptions below say what to draw, not the checked answer.

Source: PYQ Vault question bank, all six NDA Maths papers from 2024–2026, 720 questions, each one read
and sorted by hand on 2026-10-13.

Roughly another 20–25% per paper are "a diagram helps but algebra finishes it" (heights & distances,
Venn-style probability, vector algebra, conic formulas), which is weak for this format. The remaining
~65% (matrices, determinants, series, binomial, identities, DEs, most probability) belong to paper reels.

Only NDA 2026 (II) was sorted into all three groups question by question: 21 picture, 28 diagram helps,
71 algebra.

**What this means:** about half the visual questions (38 of 83) are *areas* or *corners and jumps on a
graph*, exactly what reel 005's graph code already draws. One graph template covers half the pool.

## "Why?" series: concepts first, then the PYQ (founder-approved 2026-10-17)

A "Why?" reel explains one NDA maths idea visually (~30 s, something always moving), then ends on the real
NDA question it unlocks ("NDA asked exactly this:", ~10 s). The concept widens the audience to every Class
11–12 maths student; the PYQ keeps it NDA and gives the caption its PYQ Vault link. Never a concept reel
without a real PYQ payoff (no-slop rule). On test: L13–L14 in `learnings.md`.

Chapters ranked by **how often NDA asks them** (720 Maths PYQs, six papers 2024–26, per paper) × **how
visual the idea is**:

|---|---|---|---|
| Probability | **11.8** | why 7 comes up most with two dice (diagonals → pyramid) | **#2, reel 010** |
| Matrices & Determinants | **10.0** | why a determinant is an area: "why is |2A| not 2|A|?" (rows as arrows, 1 → 2 → 4 copies) | **#3, reel 011** (25-II Q44) |
| Trig identities + Functions | 6.7 + 6.2 | why sin² + cos² = 1 (point on the unit circle + stacked bar: one grows, one shrinks) | **#4, reel 012** (25-I Q35) |
