## 1. The idea
- One sentence: an aspirant standing outside NDA today **is** the cadet who walks in one day; the sunrise turns his
  shadow into that cadet.
- Takeaway line (on screen at the end): **ONE DAY.** Chapter: n/a (defence story).
- Source: L8 (the account's 3 biggest reels, 398k / 106k / 25k, were defence stories with a question to the viewer);
  L74 (no maths reel passes ~1,950 views; a different format is one of the two ways to test past it). No PYQ.

## 2. The viewer
- An NDA aspirant, 16–19, scrolling, often muted. Knows Sudan Block on sight (it is on every NDA poster, wallpaper and
  coaching ad), so the building is recognised in the first second: **that** is the hook.
- What they feel: "that's me at the gate". The comment ask in the caption ("Which year will you walk through this
  gate?") turns the feeling into a comment.

## 3. Frame 0 (no question)
- The title **ONE DAY** (Cambria 700, 150 px, letter-spaced, white with a soft dark glow) at y ≈ 330, up 0–2.4 s and again
  from 9.6 s (hidden in between, so it never sits on the dome or the shadow).
- Sudan Block from ~90 m at pre-dawn, exposure lifted so the dome's outline reads on a phone: indigo sky, a gold glow
  behind the dome, windows lit warm, the red path and lawns. The orbit is **already moving at full speed on frame 0**
  (eases out only, F5 + F22).

## 4. Cover
- Cover at **t = 4.4 s**: behind the student at the closed gate, the dome's rim blazing gold with the sun about to clear
  it, the title added on the cover. Promises the story; the cadet twist isn't on it.
- 3:4 grid crop (y 240–1680): the title (y ≈ 330), the dome (≈ y 450–1000), the student's head and shoulders (≈ y
  1150–1600) inside.

## 7. The story without words
A grand domed building at dawn (NDA, recognised). A boy alone at its closed gate, looking up. The sun rises over the
dome and its light reaches him. Looking down, his shadow turns into a cadet with a cap and a rifle while he stands
still: the future him. The gate opens and he walks in, his cadet shadow with him. Every beat reads without the words.

## 8. Honest numbers / the model
- Sudan Block from public photos and NDA's own figures (about 132 ft = 40 m to the top, the dome about 60 ft): a long
  block of grey-brown basalt with red bands and a red roof edge, a central bay with arched ground openings, a drum with
  windows, a red ribbed dome with a finial; red path, lawns, hedges, palms. Our own model, no photo in the picture, no
  crest. The way the real front faces isn't claimed: the caption won't say "sunrise at NDA".
- Geometry (tested): the student stands 120 m from the building's front. The sun rises straight behind the dome; it
  clears the dome top (as seen from his eyes, 1.6 m) at 4.8 s, when its elevation is atan(38.4 / 120) ≈ 17.7°. At that
  moment the dome's shadow ends exactly at his feet (the same line); the sun goes on to 21°, so the dome's shadow ends
  ≈ 16 m short of him. His shadow = 1.75 m ÷ tan(21°) ≈ 4.6 m, pointing straight at the camera.

## 9. Risks
- **The figure reads as a doll.** Backlit, from behind, silhouette with a rim light; no face; the walk is 3 short steps.
- **The cadet reads as a guard.** Cap + rifle at the slope = passing-out parade; checked on the stills.
- **The change isn't noticed.** It's the only thing moving 5.6–7.0, centre frame, with a rising shimmer; held 1.2 s.
- **Dizzy orbit.** One turn, easing out; ~90 m.
- **Title on the dome.** Title shown only when a test says the finial is below y 460.
- **Sudan Block not recognised.** Dome + drum proportions and the red roof edge first, checked against the reference.
