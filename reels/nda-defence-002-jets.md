## What's true (checked, F42)

Every line with its source is in the storyboard's § 8 (PIB):
- Tejas: DRDO-developed, built by HAL. Mirage 2000: a French aircraft, upgraded under contracts with French firms and HAL
  (PIB 2011; FOC 2018). Su-30MKI: built by HAL under licence (India–Russia agreement, 2000). Rafale: formally inducted
  10 Sep 2020 at Ambala. All four flew at Vayu Shakti-2026 (PIB).
- How to spot each: Tejas a small compound ("kinked") triangle wing; Mirage 2000 a plain triangle wing with a straight
  edge; Su-30MKI two tail fins (and front wings and tail wings); Rafale front wings (canards) and one tail fin. Our models
  are simplified, not to scale (said in the caption); no roundels, badges or tail numbers.
