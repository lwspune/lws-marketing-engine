# @soundmindwithshivani learnings log

Kept apart from the LWS logs (`reels/learnings.md`, `reels/pyqvault-learnings.md`): an outside brand, a different
audience and different formats (carousels and "ME" story reels). Same method: every lesson from real numbers becomes a
**testable idea**: what we saw, what we think it means, which posts test it, and the result. Update **Result** and
**Status** after each post's 7-day numbers (GoPost: the post's Done card, or Insights). A point in `framework.md` (the
S-numbers) changes only through its change log. Lessons are grouped like the framework: both formats, carousels, reels.

**Sound Mind's numbers are not in this repo** (an outside brand's data; CLAUDE.md: write as if the repo were public).
The baselines and each lesson's figures go in **`analytics/soundmind-baseline.md`** (local only, git-ignored); a Result
here says what happened in words ("above the typical carousel on shares"), never the figures.

All three logs share **one numbering** (L1, L2, …), so a number always means one lesson. The next new lesson is **L68** (L64 and L65 are in the LWS logs),
in whichever log it belongs to.

**Status:** 🧪 testing · ✅ confirmed (do it from now on) · ❌ rejected (don't) · 📌 fact (no test needed)

Winning is judged per format (`framework.md`): a **carousel** against the typical carousel on shares per 1,000 reached
(at least 2 shares); a **reel** against the typical reel on reach and watch time first, then shares. At 7 days.
