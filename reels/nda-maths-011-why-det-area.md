## The idea (checked)

- The rows of A = [3 1; 1 2] are the arrows (3, 1) and (1, 2). |A| = 3·2 − 1·1 = **5** = the area of the
  parallelogram they span.
- Multiply one row by 2 → that side doubles → the parallelogram is 2 copies of the original → area 10
  (Statement II: one row × m ⇒ det × m).
- 2A multiplies **both** rows by 2 → 4 copies → |2A| = 20 = **4|A|**, not 2|A|. An n×n matrix has n rows, so
  |mA| = m·m⋯m |A| = **mⁿ|A|** (Statement I).
- NDA 2025 (II) Q44 asks whether both statements are true and whether II explains I: II applied to each of
  the n rows gives I, so **(a)**. (b) is the trap: both true, but missing that one causes the other.
