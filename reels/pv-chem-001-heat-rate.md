## The chemistry (checked)

- Speed ∝ √T: 298 → 308 K makes molecules √(308/298) − 1 = 1.7% faster (live readout), yet the rate doubles.
- Energy distribution f(E) ∝ √E·e^(−E/kT). For +10 K to double the rate, Eₐ ≈ ln 2 / (1 − 298/308) ≈ 21.35 kT.
  The fraction past Eₐ is ~10⁻⁹ of all molecules, so it's shown in a **labelled ×10⁹ zoom**; the main curves
  are drawn to scale (they really do look almost the same). Tail area ratio 308 K / 298 K, computed: ×2.0.
- No fake counts: the jar shows molecules moving (speed scales with √T); it never claims to count reactions.
