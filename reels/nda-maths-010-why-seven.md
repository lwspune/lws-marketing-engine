## The idea (checked)

- Two dice: 6 × 6 = **36** equally likely outcomes. In the grid, cell (a, b) holds a + b, so each sum is one
  diagonal.
- Ways per sum: 2→1, 3→2, 4→3, 5→4, 6→5, **7→6**, 8→5, 9→4, 10→3, 11→2, 12→1 (total 36). 7's diagonal is the
  longest, so 7 is the most likely sum: 6/36 = 1/6.
- NDA 2026 (II) Q111: sum 5 = (1,4), (2,3), (3,2), (4,1) → 4 ways; sum 7 → 6 ways; they can't happen together.
  36 − 4 − 6 = 26 → P = 26/36 = **13/18** → **(c)**. Trap: (d) 5/6 = 30/36 removes only the 7s.
